Second income path

Earn without depositing or trading

Copying a strategy is the main way to use this site, and it needs money in an account. This is the other way: become a broker partner and get paid a share of what the people you refer generate. Neither broker asks a partner to fund an account or place a trade.

How it works

Four steps, no deposit

1 · Register as a partner

Open a free partner account with the broker. LiteFinance also asks you to verify your profile with ID and proof of address before your link appears.

2 · Get your links

The partner cabinet gives you a referral link, plus a separate link for each copy-trading strategy on RoboForex. LiteFinance adds banners, landing pages and a partner app.

3 · Share them honestly

Write, post or explain trading to people who already want to trade or copy. Every person who signs up through your link is tied to you.

4 · Get paid on their activity

When your referrals trade, or copy a strategy that profits, the broker credits you a share. You never touch their money and they pay nothing extra.

Ways to earn

What you can be paid for

Trading referrals

A share of spread and swap

RoboForex pays up to 85% of the spread on your referrals' closed trades — about $8.80 per lot of gold or EURUSD — and 30% of the swap on positions they hold overnight. LiteFinance pays 70% of its profit from their trades once they trade 100+ lots or deposit $5,000+ in a month, 30% below that.

Copy-trading referrals

A share of the trader's fee

Refer someone who copies a strategy, and when that strategy earns its performance fee you receive part of it. On RoboForex: investor profit × trader's fee (10–50%) × partner share (10–50%), paid by the trader, credited as soon as the trader is paid.

Per sign-up · and partners of partners

Flat fees and sub-partners

LiteFinance's CPS plan pays $50 per referral who deposits $100+ and then makes 50 trades totalling 2+ lots. On both brokers, anyone you refer who becomes a partner is your sub-partner, and you earn 10% of what they earn.

Before you start

Rules and honest limits

▸
No self-referralYou, your relatives and your own companies cannot be your referrals. LiteFinance says so outright; assume every broker enforces it.
▸
Country restrictionsRoboForex and its partners do not target clients in the EU/EEA/UK, the USA, Canada, Japan, Australia and other listed countries; LiteFinance does not serve the EEA, USA, Israel, Russia and others. Check where your audience lives.
▸
Which accounts payRoboForex calculates partner commission on MT4/MT5 Pro and R StocksTrader accounts. LiteFinance counts Classic, ECN and Cent. Hedged positions can reduce LiteFinance commission.
▸
Earnings follow their tradingNo trades, no income. Rates are “up to”, can differ by region and can change without notice; confirm them in the partner cabinet.
▸
The conflict of interestYou are paid when people trade, and most retail traders lose money. Recommend only what you would use yourself, show the risks, and never promise returns.

Questions

Partner FAQ

Do I need to deposit or trade to become a partner?

No deposit or trading requirement appears on either broker's partner pages. You register, open a partner account and share your link. LiteFinance asks you to verify your client profile (ID and proof of address) before your referral link appears.

What exactly am I paid for?

For the trading of the people you refer. RoboForex pays up to 85% of the spread on their closed trades and 30% of the swap on their open positions. LiteFinance pays 30–70% of its profit from their trades, or a flat $50 per qualifying referral on its CPS plan. If your referrals do not trade, you earn nothing.

Can I earn from copy trading without being a trader?

Yes. On RoboForex, a partner whose referral copies a strategy receives a share of that strategy's performance fee: investor profit × trader's fee × partner's share. The trader pays it, not the investor on top. LiteFinance describes the same idea: you earn part of a trader's profit when your referral copies a trader who has set a partner percentage.

What are sub-partners?

People you refer who become partners themselves. Both brokers pay you 10% of what your sub-partners earn, on top of your own referrals.

Can I refer myself or my family?

No. LiteFinance states that neither a partner nor relatives or affiliated entities may act as a referral. Treat the same as true everywhere: self-referral is the fastest way to have a partner account closed.

Which accounts count on RoboForex?

RoboForex states that partner commission is calculated on MT4/MT5 Pro and R StocksTrader accounts. Its partner pages do not mention cent (ProCent) accounts, so confirm in the partner cabinet before steering referrals to them.
