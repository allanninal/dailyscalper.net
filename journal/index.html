Why this account

Why RoboForex ProCent

A small balance only grows if costs do not eat it. ProCent is RoboForex's cent account: it trades the same gold price in cents, with no commission, and with RoboForex's Swap-Free option it pays no overnight fee either. The two figures below come from this account's own trades, not from the broker's brochure.

Commission paid

0.00¢

Across all 20 closed trades. ProCent charges no per-lot commission; the cost of a trade is the spread.

Swap paid

0.00¢

Including 2 trades held through the daily rollover. On a standard ProCent account gold carries a swap of -60 points long and +19 short; this account was opened with Swap-Free.

Cent units

1,600¢ = $16

Balances and profits are in US cents, so a $16 account can run proper position sizing: a 0.01 lot gold trade moves about 1¢ per $1 move in gold.

What it still costs: the spread. RoboForex's specifications list an average ProCent gold spread of 18 points, paid on every entry, so a scalper has to beat that on each trade. Swap-Free is an option chosen when the account is opened, is subject to RoboForex's terms and may not be offered in every region.
